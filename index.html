Chester, California

Cedar Lodge Motel & RV Park

Three ways to stay: a motel room, a whole home, or a full hookup RV pad, three miles west of Chester. Where Highway 36 meets Highway 89, a mile from Lake Almanor (trout fishing all year) and close to Lassen Volcanic National Park. Open all year.

Motel rooms each have a kitchenette, private bath and their own door onto the lot. The home sleeps up to 9, with a full kitchen, fenced back yard, and washer and dryer. The 15 gravel RV sites have 20, 30 and 50 amp power, water and sewer, plus a guest shower facility. Pets: $20 per pet per night in rooms, capped at $60; free at RV sites, up to two.

Each motel room sleeps up to four, and the Pacific Crest Trail crosses Highway 36 west of Chester, making it a good rest stop for hikers.

  • Motel rooms with kitchenettes
  • 3 bed / 2 bath home
  • 15 full-hookup RV sites
  • Pets welcome

October offer: 20% off motel rooms for October nights with code OCT20.
